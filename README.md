## Thibaut Misme personal webpage
I am a PhD student at Trinity College Dublin (TCD) in Ireland (2023-2027).
My work lies in Computational Number Theory. I am especially interested in explicit 2-descent for Jacobians of general curves and Theta Characteristics.

Visit my [institution page](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/people/mismet/).

## PARI/GP
For the sake of my research, I implement in PARI/GP, an open, free, number theory focused and efficient language. It includes a high level version that is designed for both involved and quick implementation. Visit their [webpage](https://pari.math.u-bordeaux.fr/) for more infomations.
